# SPEC-011: Demo Specification
| Version | 1.0.0 | Owner | Platform Product | Status | Proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dependencies | SPEC-005, SPEC-007, SPEC-009 | Related ADRs | ADR-009 | Related contracts | CONTRACT-001–007 |
## Purpose
Specify one deterministic proof of the platform lifecycle.
## Scope
Community AI Lab only.
## Requirements
The demo SHALL use the fixed 25-laptop scenario and show Need, Fund Intel Recommendation, Human Approval, Allocation, Purchase, Evidence, Receipt, Verification, Impact, and Donor Notification in order.
## Non-goals
No live payment, vendor, or alternate demo is required.
