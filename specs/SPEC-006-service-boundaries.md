# SPEC-006: Service Boundaries
| Version | 1.0.0 | Owner | Platform Architecture | Status | Proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dependencies | SPEC-002, SPEC-005 | Related ADRs | ADR-010 | Related contracts | CONTRACT-001–007 |
## Purpose
Assign ecosystem responsibilities without prescribing deployed services.
## Scope
Fund Intel, Autonomous Giving, Impact Relay, and future services.
## Requirements
Fund Intel observes, normalizes, and recommends only. Autonomous Giving governs, approves, and allocates only. Impact Relay collects evidence, verifies, and notifies only.
## Non-goals
Repository layout, network topology, and operational ownership are excluded.
