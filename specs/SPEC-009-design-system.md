# SPEC-009: Design System
| Version | 1.0.0 | Owner | Platform Product | Status | Proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dependencies | SPEC-004, SPEC-011 | Related ADRs | ADR-009 | Related contracts | CONTRACT-007 |
## Purpose
Specify audit-visible information design.
## Scope
Typography, spacing, color, motion, iconography, diagrams, documentation formatting, and component naming.
## Requirements
Lifecycle, approval, evidence, and verification state SHALL be explicit and accessible; users SHALL trace impact to evidence and allocation.
## Non-goals
No UI code, tokens, components, or brand artwork is provided.
