# ADR-010: Future Services

| Status | Proposed |
| --- | --- |
| Date | 2026-08-03 |
| Related specs | SPEC-005 |

## Context

New services must interoperate without redistributing platform responsibility.

## Decision

Admit future services through owned contracts and the responsibility boundaries in SPEC-005.

## Consequences

Service count may change; platform invariants do not.
